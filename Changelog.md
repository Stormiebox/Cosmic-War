## [v4.0.3]

### 🪲 Confirmed Defects Fixed

- [Bugfix] **Signal Jamming Net Could Trap A Player Indefinitely In An Allied Faction's Own Territory, With No Hostiles Present (`cw_signal_jamming_net.lua`):** This event's `spawn()` only checks that the sector's controller is a `cw_enabled` AI faction — it never checks that faction's relationship to the player, so it could roll in a sector controlled by a faction the player is Allied, at Ceasefire, or simply at peace with. Once it did, `updateServer()`'s `ship:blockHyperspace(2.5)` re-applied to *every* player physically present in the sector every 2 seconds, with no relation check at all — unlike the drones' own combat AI, which already correctly declines to fire on a non-hostile player via normal faction relations. The result: a player could be blocked from jumping out of a friendly ally's sector, with nothing hostile to fight, and no way out short of destroying that ally's own defensive drones (which would have damaged the relationship further, for a debuff that shouldn't have applied to them in the first place) — exactly the reported "flew 200km away and still jammed, no hostiles, can't leave" symptom (sector-wide hyperspace jams aren't distance-based, and Avorion sectors have no sublight route out — only a jump crosses the boundary). Fixed by only jamming players whose `Player:getRelationStatus()` against the controlling faction is `RelationStatus.War`, matching the event's own "fight your way out of hostile territory" framing.
- [Bugfix] **Artillery Barrage Could Shield-Jam Players Allied With The Attacking Faction, Not Just Its Enemies (`cw_artillery_barrage.lua`):** Same missing-relation-check shape as the Signal Jamming Net fix above, caught by the same sibling sweep. The platform belongs to `attackerFactionIndex` and its shield-suppression re-application loop (`updateServer()`, every 8 seconds) applied to every player present in the sector regardless of their standing with that faction — an ally of the attacker (or simply anyone not at war with them) had their own shields suppressed by a platform that was never targeting them. Fixed the same way: only re-apply the jammer to players whose `Player:getRelationStatus()` against `attackerFactionIndex` is `RelationStatus.War`.

## [v4.0.2]

### 🪲 Confirmed Defects Fixed

- [Bugfix] **Scorched Earth's Target Sector Never Actually Contained Enough of the Required Material (`cw_scorched_earth.lua`):** v4.0.1 fixed the instant-completion trigger, but that only made the *check* honest — it didn't fix what the sector actually contained. `spawnEvent()`'s `generator:createAsteroidField(0.2)` populates its resource-bearing asteroids by rolling each one's material through `AsteroidFieldGenerator:getAsteroidType()`, a weighted-random pick across every material plausible at that location (`Balancing_GetMaterialProbability`) — never specifically the contract's own pre-chosen `matType`. A player could mine out every resource asteroid the field spawned and still never find enough of — or any of — the one material the contract actually required, exactly matching the report that mining out the whole sector didn't move the tracker. Fixed by spawning a dedicated cluster of 20 guaranteed-material asteroids on top of the ambient field, via `AsteroidFieldGenerator:createSmallAsteroid()` — the same function vanilla's own field generation calls internally — built explicitly from the contract's required `Material`, so the requirement is always actually achievable regardless of what the ambient field happened to roll.
- [Bugfix] **Scorched Earth's Patrols Spawned Immediately Instead of After a Grace Window, Contradicting Its Own Briefing (`cw_scorched_earth.lua`):** The mission text has always read "strip-mine ... before their patrols arrive," but `spawnEvent()` spawned all three Defender ships in the same breath as the asteroid field, the instant the player arrived — there was never actually a window to mine before patrols showed up. Split ship-spawning out into a new `spawnPatrols()`, fired from a new phase trigger 45 seconds after arrival (`PATROL_DELAY`), gated on the player still actually being in the target sector when the delay elapses (`ShipGenerator.createDefender` reads the *current* sector for its turret/volume balancing, so firing this while the player is elsewhere would have spawned hostile ships into whatever sector they happened to be in instead — the trigger simply waits for their return rather than doing that).
- [Bugfix] **Salvage Race's Wreckage Field Was Almost Never Actually Salvageable (`cw_salvagerace.lua`):** Two compounding defects. First, vanilla's own `SectorGenerator:createWreckage()` rolls a real ~90% chance per piece (`1 - chanceForUnstrippedWreckage`, default `0.1 * resourceWreckageFactor`) to strip it down to bare Hull blocks with zero harvestable material via `ShipUtility.stripWreckage()` — fine for ambient battlefield debris, fatal for a "salvage X material from this field" mechanic, since on average only half of one of the five spawned pieces would even keep its resources. Second, even an unstripped piece was no help: `createWreckage()` builds its plan via `PlanGenerator.makeShipPlan`/`makeFreighterPlan` with no material argument, which falls back to the *spawning faction's* own default material tier, unrelated to the contract's own pre-chosen `matType`. Together, this meant the only things actually worth salvaging in the sector were the wrecks left behind by destroying the two Rival Scavenger ships in combat — exactly matching the report that the dedicated wreckage field itself was never really there. Fixed by building each wreck's plan directly from the contract's required material and spawning via `createUnstrippedWreckage()` — the same function `createWreckage()` calls internally, minus its strip roll — called on the mission's own generator instance so it isn't subject to a separate, unrelated bug in `createWreckage()`'s internal relay (it invokes its sibling as `SectorGenerator:createUnstrippedWreckage(...)` — through the class table — instead of `self:...`, though that particular slip doesn't affect this fix since it's bypassed entirely by calling `createUnstrippedWreckage()` directly).
- [Bugfix] **Sector Raid Terminated Immediately On Accept For Most Players (`cw_sector_raid.lua`):** Present, unchanged, since this contract's original v3.3.0 introduction — not a v4.0.0-era regression. `findEnemySectorWithStations()` searched a 2-20 radius centered on the PLAYER's own current position (wherever they happened to accept the bulletin) for a sector actually controlled by the specific enemy faction and containing a station. The enemy faction's territory has no reason to be anywhere near the giver faction's own space, so that search frequently found nothing at all, hitting `initialize()`'s `if not targetX or not targetY then terminate() return end` the instant the contract was accepted — matching the report exactly ("disappears soon as you accept it"). Fixed by centering the search on the enemy faction's own home sector instead (`enemyFaction:getHomeSectorCoordinates()`), the same fix pattern `cw_armistice_escort.lua` and `cw_corridor_interdiction.lua` already use for this exact "find a sector relative to a specific faction" problem. Also removed a redundant second `Faction(enemyIndex)` lookup later in the same function, left over from before this fix needed the faction object earlier — the same "Redundant Double Faction() Lookup" class the v4.0.0 pass already cleaned up in 21 other mission files' reward calculations, just at a different call site this file's own pass missed.
- [Bugfix] **Sensor Deployment Could Never Complete Past The First Buoy (`cw_sensor_deployment.lua`):** Present, unchanged, since this contract's original v3.3.0 introduction. This is the only War Contract in the mod whose single completion trigger has to fire three separate times — once per deployed buoy, advancing `mission.data.location` to the next target sector each time. `structuredmission.lua`'s own trigger dispatch (`updatePhaseTriggers()`) sets `trigger.triggered = true` after *any* successful callback and then permanently stops re-evaluating that trigger's condition unless `repeating = true` is explicitly set on it — this trigger never set it. Buoy #1 deployed correctly (the trigger's first, only permitted firing); flying to the second target sector, getting within 500m, and doing whatever the player tried from there could never register, because the trigger had already stopped being checked at all. Fixed by adding `repeating = true`; the trigger's own existing per-sector `deployed_x_y` guard already prevents re-awarding a sector that's already done, so this doesn't risk a double-fire. Also added a live "Buoys deployed: X/3" counter to the contract's own description line, updated in the same callback, since a 3-stage objective with no running count was exactly the kind of invisible-progress gap the mining/salvage counters earlier in this version were added to close. Players already carrying a stuck copy of this contract should have it start working again automatically after their next server restart (or full relog) once the mod update is installed — `mission.phases[1].triggers` is a plain table literal at file scope, rebuilt fresh (with `triggered` unset) every time the mission script reloads, and each sector's already-earned buoy stays recorded in `mission.data.custom`, which does persist. If it doesn't, abandoning and re-accepting the contract from the same giver's bulletin board is a clean, guaranteed reset.

### ⭐ New Features

- [Feature] **Live Mining/Salvage Progress Readout (`cw_scorched_earth.lua`, `cw_salvagerace.lua`):** Both contracts' tracked objective bullet now reads "Mining ${material}: ${progress}/${amount}" (Scorched Earth) or "Salvaging ${material}: ${progress}/${amount}" (Salvage Race) once the player arrives, instead of a static "head to sector" line that never changed until the contract completed outright. A new `mission.phases[1].updateServer` hook (a real `structuredmission.lua` lifecycle callback, dispatched only server-side and polled once a second by default) recomputes `current - baselineAmount` — the same delta the completion trigger already checks — clamps it to the required amount, and only calls `sync()` when the displayed number actually changes, so idle time between mining runs doesn't spam a sync every second.
- [Feature] **Live Gather Progress Readout Extended to Four Delivery Contracts (`cw_resource_heist.lua`, `cw_relief_convoy.lua`, `cw_medical_airlift.lua`, `cw_refugeeresettlement.lua`):** Same live counter, extended to every other "gather X material" contract in the mod. These four don't use a baseline snapshot the way Scorched Earth/Salvage Race do — their own completion trigger checks raw current stock (the player can gather from anywhere, even before accepting, and hands the material over on delivery via `payResource()`), so their counter shows that same raw current stock, staying exactly consistent with what actually completes the contract rather than introducing a second, different notion of "progress." Resource Heist's counter lives on its dynamically-inserted "return with cargo" bullet (which only exists once the player has reached the target sector once), gated on the same `mission.data.custom.spawned` flag its own completion trigger already uses; the other three show it on their single delivery bullet from the moment the contract is accepted. Deliberately not extended to kill-count events (Prisoner Transport's escorts, Signal Jamming Net's drones, etc.) — remaining enemy ships are already visible on-screen, and a periodic chat broadcast for that would reintroduce the notification spam Cosmic Overhaul's own economy-message toggle was just built to let players turn off.

## [v4.0.1]

### 🪲 Confirmed Defects Fixed

- [Bugfix] **Scorched Earth and Salvage Race Completed Instantly On Arrival, No Mining Required (`cw_scorched_earth.lua`, `cw_salvagerace.lua`):** Both contracts' completion trigger checked `player:getResources()[matType] >= requiredAmount` — the player's total current stock of the target material — the moment the trigger polled after `onTargetLocationEntered`. Unlike the mod's delivery contracts (Resource Heist, Relief Convoy, Medical Airlift, Refugee Resettlement), which call `player:payResource()` and so correctly treat pre-existing cargo as a legitimate delivery, neither Scorched Earth nor Salvage Race ever takes the material away — the player keeps it, by design ("Keep it -- denying them the resources is the mission"). So a player already carrying enough of the target material (common ores like Iron or Titanium are frequent cargo) completed the contract on the very first trigger poll after entering the sector, without mining or salvaging anything. Fixed by snapshotting the player's resource count for the target material at the moment they arrive (`mission.data.custom.baselineAmount`, set in `onTargetLocationEntered`) and requiring `(current - baselineAmount) >= requiredAmount` instead — the material now has to be genuinely gained after arrival.
- [Bugfix] **Five New Galaxy Events Left Their Spawned Ships (And Their Own Sector Scripts) Running Forever If A Player Left Before Resolving Them (`cw_artillery_barrage.lua`, `cw_mutiny.lua`, `cw_prisoner_transport.lua`, `cw_runner_intercept.lua`, `cw_signal_jamming_net.lua`):** Every comparable event elsewhere in this mod (Fleet Clash, Distress Beacon Trap, and this same update's own Coalition Muster and Field Hospital Convoy) attaches vanilla's `data/scripts/entity/deleteonplayersleft.lua` to every ship it spawns, specifically so an unresolved encounter cleans itself up once no player remains in the sector. These five files spawned their tracked combat ships (Artillery Barrage's platform; Mutiny's loyalist escorts and mutineer; Prisoner Transport's transport and escorts; Runner Intercept's runner and interceptors; Signal Jamming Net's drones) without it. A player who triggered one of these events and then jumped away without finishing it left those ships alive in the sector indefinitely, and left the event's own `updateServer` polling loop running forever waiting for a resolution that would never come. All five now attach `deleteonplayersleft.lua` to every ship they spawn, matching the rest of the mod.
- [Bugfix] **Six Player-Facing Payment Notifications Resolved Against The Server's Own Locale Instead Of Each Player's (`cw_checkpoint_picket.lua`, `cw_defector_ship.lua`, `tradingpost.lua` x3, `militaryoutpost.lua`):** The same translation-boundary defect as the v4.0.0 "Fourteen Server-Side Chat/Title Strings" fix, just on a different API: `player:pay(description, amount)`'s `description` argument is documented (`Player [Server]` stub) as "an economy notification... sent to the player" — real, rendered, player-visible text, not inert bookkeeping. All six call sites (Border Toll, Defection Payment, Sanctions Relief Brokerage, Diplomatic Aid Package, Warbond Purchase, Letter of Marque Repairs) marked that description with `%_t`, which resolves the string immediately via `GetLocalizedString()` against whatever locale is active in the server's own Lua state, instead of `%_T`, which defers translation to each receiving client. Confirmed against vanilla precedent: every one of vanilla's own ~40 `:pay()` call sites with a string description uses `%_T`, none use `%_t`. All six corrected to `%_T`.

## [v4.0.0] The Complete Overhaul

*A ground-up rebalancing and expansion pass: every confirmed defect fixed, numbers realigned to their own documented intent, two disconnected territorial-expansion systems unified into one, four new systems (Intelligence Network, Humanitarian Contracts, War Score & Attrition, Coalition Ceasefires) built on top, and five mechanics with no vanilla Avorion equivalent (Frontlines, War Weariness, Supply Lines, Letters of Marque, Occupation & Insurgency) closing out the pass.*

### 🪲 Confirmed Defects Fixed

- [Bugfix] **Mercantile's Documented Nerf Never Reached The Missions (22 mission files):** v3.0.11 documented cutting the Mercantile payout bonus from 3x to 1.5x. `cosmicwar_mercenary.lua` and the trait's own tooltip in `cosmicwartraits.lua` ("Pays 50% extra") already reflected 1.5x, but all 22 War Contract mission files still computed `and 3 or 1`. Corrected across all 22 files. This restores already-documented intent; it is not a new balance decision.
- [Bugfix] **Four War Zone Events Remained Completely Ungated (`cw_stationsiege.lua`, `cw_capital_ship_duel.lua`, `cw_orbital_bombardment.lua`; `cw_distress_beacon_trap.lua` already fixed in v3.4.5):** None of the three checked `neutral_zone`, sector ownership, or that the "attacker" was even an AI faction. `cw_capital_ship_duel.lua` in particular never verified its two "duelists" were different, hostile, non-player factions — `Galaxy():getNearestFaction()` could resolve the same faction, or a player's own faction/alliance, for both spawn points. All three now resolve the sector's controlling faction as the defender and that defender's registered `enemy_faction` as the attacker, matching the pattern every other roaming event in this mod already uses, and bail out cleanly (with `terminate()`) whenever no valid hostile pair exists.
- [Bugfix] **Decapitation Strike's "Massive Super Boss" Was Stock Carrier Size (`cw_decapitationstrike.lua`):** `ShipGenerator.createCarrier(faction, pos, 10.0)`'s third argument was believed to scale hull volume; it's actually the fighter-squadron count, and 10 is that function's own default — confirmed against `shipgenerator.lua`, which hardcodes carrier volume internally with no override hook at all. Since hull size genuinely cannot be scaled through this generator function, toughness is now scaled instead via `Durability.maxDurabilityFactor` and `addBaseMultiplier(StatsBonuses.ShieldDurability, ...)` — the same officially-supported APIs already used throughout this mod (not the raw `ship.maxDurability` write that caused the v3.0.5 crash class) — using the same `hpMult` value that already scaled FireRate and Boarding defense.
- [Bugfix] **Six Missions Carried A Stale, Mismatched Inner Reward-Tier Floor (`cw_deploy_mines.lua`, `cw_propaganda_broadcast.lua`, `cw_assassinate_general.lua`, `cw_supply_line_raid.lua`, `cw_extract_pow.lua`, `cw_champion_duel.lua`):** Each defined a `getBulletin()` floor that didn't match its real outer gate in `missionbulletins.lua` — harmless today (the outer gate always wins first) but a latent trap. All six realigned to their actual tier.
- [Bugfix] **Sector Raid Missing A Sibling Flag (`cw_sector_raid.lua`):** Set `noBossEncountersTargetSector` but not `noPlayerEventsTargetSector`, unlike every other War Contract. Added.
- [Bugfix] **Every War Bulletin Has Said "[Balanced]" Since v3.0.0 (`cosmicwarnews.lua`):** `cosmicwartraits.lua` deliberately strips the legacy `cw_diplomatic_stance` field to `nil` when converting to the formal custom-trait system, but the news bulletin's bracketed stance tag still read that exact dead field, silently falling back to "Balanced" on every single headline regardless of the faction's real trait. Fixed by deriving the display label from the faction's actual assigned custom trait, reading its display name from the same Cosmic Vault trait registry (`CosmicVaultFaction.getCustomTraits()`) the vanilla UI itself uses — rather than a second hardcoded label list that could drift from `cosmicwartraits.lua`'s registrations.
- [Bugfix] **Imperialist/Entrenched Expansion Had No Collision Check (`cosmicwarexpansion.lua`):** Rolled a random offset within a radius and claimed it if unclaimed at the moment of the roll — but never verified the target stayed unclaimed by the time `expandToSector` actually ran, and (for Imperialist specifically) picked an arbitrary point rather than growing contiguously from owned territory. Replaced with a directional walk matching Cosmic Ascendancy's own `ca_expansion_manager.lua`: pick a heading, walk outward one sector at a time, claim the first unclaimed sector found, and give up (not skip past) if the ray runs into someone else's territory first. Entrenched's smaller-radius "fortify core territory" logic keeps its simpler random-point approach (with the collision check applied) since it's backfilling already-adjacent territory, not pushing a frontier.
- [Bugfix] **In-Game Codex Had Two Stale Claims (`infoCw.lua`, `WIKI.md`):** Shield Jammer was still documented as "20 seconds" at a blanket 35% — the real numbers (10 seconds, 35% sieges / 15% fleet clashes) have been correct in `WIKI.md`'s Architecture Summary since the v3.0.8 nerf, just not propagated to the Codex text. Also both files cited a nonexistent `player/background/cosmicwardiplomacy.lua` for Persistent Diplomacy Drift; the real file is `server/background/cosmicwardiplomacy.lua`, wired through `galaxy/server.lua`. Both corrected in both files.
- [Cleanup] **Dead Local Removed (`cosmicwarstatus.lua`):** `local galaxy = Galaxy()` was read only for its nil-check and never referenced again. Collapsed to `if not Galaxy() then`.
- [Re-verified, not a defect] **"Two Disconnected War Heat Sources":** Previously flagged as a concern in this project's own overhaul plan. Re-read `cosmicwarbridge.lua` and `cw_fleetclash.lua` directly before touching anything: `CosmicWarBridge.getFactionWarHeat(idx)` is a one-line wrapper returning `getWarHeatSnapshot()[idx]` — the exact same canonical snapshot every other reader uses, just a single-key lookup. No code change was needed.
- [Re-verified, not a defect] **"Hero of the People Mechanically Inert":** The original v3.1.0 "+10% trade prices" promise was already corrected to an honest, non-mechanical status message in v3.4.0, and neither WIKI.md, PLAYER_GUIDE.md, nor infoCw.lua's current text repeated the old overclaim. See the "Hero of the People Now Pays A Real Reward" entry below for what changed instead — a deliberate v4.0.0 improvement, not a bugfix.
- [Bugfix] **The "Three Highest-Tier Contracts Realigned" Fix Below Never Actually Reached Players (`cw_assassinate_general.lua`, `cw_supply_line_raid.lua`, `cw_extract_pow.lua`):** Caught during the extended-pass re-audit. Each file's `initialize()` did carry the corrected formula (see the Numbers & Balance entry below), but `initialize()` only falls back to computing it when no precomputed reward was passed in — and the standard mission-board accept path (`bulletinboard.lua`'s `player:addScript(bulletin.script, unpack(bulletin.arguments))`) always passes one, built by each file's own `getBulletin()` from the *old*, unfixed formula. `initialize()`'s corrected formula was therefore unreachable dead code the entire time; every contract accepted from a bulletin board still paid the original underpriced amount. Fixed by applying the same formula correction to `getBulletin()` in all three files, matching what `initialize()` already had.
- [Bugfix] **Siege Event Crashed Every Client Who Entered A Contested Sector (`siegeevent.lua`):** `SiegeEvent.initialize()` had no `onServer()`/`onClient()` guard at all, unlike its sibling event scripts (`cw_fleetclash.lua`, `cw_eclipse_vanguard.lua`), and its very first call was `CosmicVaultTerritory.getContestedZones()` — a function Cosmic Vault only defines inside a top-level `if onServer() then ... end` block, so on the client it's `nil`, not a callable no-op. Every client who entered a contested sector crashed immediately with `attempt to call a nil value`. Added `if onClient() then return end` at the top of `initialize()`, matching the established sibling pattern.
- [Bugfix] **Eclipse Vanguard's Warning Banner Never Rendered (`cw_eclipse_vanguard.lua`):** `spawn()` broadcast a client RPC (`showVanguardBanner`) whose handler then called `CosmicVaultUI.ShowCinematicBanner(Player(), ...)` on itself — but `ShowCinematicBanner` is a server-only "push" helper (`if not onServer() then return false end`, its real work is its own internal `player:invokeFunction` push) meant to be called *from the server* targeting a specific player, not called by a client on itself. It silently returned `false` and did nothing, every time, for every player, since this event was added. Now called directly from the server inside `spawn()`, looping `Sector():getPlayers()`.
- [Bugfix] **Subspace Containment Contract Could Never Be Offered (`missionbulletins.lua`):** a fully implemented 0.80-heat-tier War Contract (`cw_subspace_containment.lua` — `getBulletin()`, phases, and rewards all present and correctly patterned) was missing from `MissionBulletins.getPossibleMissions()`'s `heat >= 0.80` registration list, unlike every one of its tier-mates (High-Value Defection, Assassinate General, Supply Line Raid, Blockade Runner). It could never actually be offered on a bulletin board, despite `infoCw.lua`'s in-game Codex describing it as available. Registered at `prob = 0.5`, matching its siblings.
- [Bugfix] **Weather-Assisted Boarding Synergy Never Triggered (`cw_frontlinesiege.lua`):** compared `CosmicVaultWeather.getWeatherAt(x, y)`'s return value directly against string literals (`"IonStorm"`/`"DarkMatterFog"`), but the function returns a table (`{x, y, type, expiry}`) or `nil`, never a bare string — the comparison was unconditionally false, so the mission's own documented "boarding defense halved during Ion Storms/Dark Matter Fog" synergy never fired. Fixed to compare `weather.type` on a non-nil weather table.
- [Bugfix] **Dead "Wealthy"/"Poor" Faction Trait Tags Never Displayed (`galacticpolitics_tab.lua`):** the Galactic Politics tooltip checked `faction:getTrait("wealthy")` and `("poor")` — neither is a real vanilla Avorion trait key (the confirmed set is `aggressive/peaceful/careful/brave/generous/greedy/opportunistic/honorable/trusting/mistrustful`), so `Faction:getTrait()` returned `0.0` for both, permanently, and no faction ever showed a "Wealthy"/"Poor" tag. Removed the two dead checks.
- [Bugfix] **Elite Headhunter Damage Scaling Used An Unproven Stat Lever (`cw_eventscheduler.lua`):** the bounty-hunter ambush ship's damage scaling was applied solely via `ship.damageMultiplier = ... * 2.5` — a real property whose effect on the actual damage-output pipeline is unconfirmed (durability was correctly, separately scaled via `Durability.maxDurabilityFactor`). Every other boss/elite-scaling call site in this mod uses the proven `addBaseMultiplier(StatsBonuses.FireRate, ...)` lever instead; this was the one spot that didn't. Added the equivalent `addBaseMultiplier(StatsBonuses.FireRate, 1.5)` call for the same intended 2.5x total.
- [Bugfix] **Final Pass — Salvage Race Could Never Be Offered (`missionbulletins.lua`):** a fully implemented 0.35-heat-tier War Contract, complete with its own in-game Codex article, was missing from `MissionBulletins.getPossibleMissions()`'s registration list entirely — the exact same class of defect as the Subspace Containment fix above, in the same registry, caught this time by diffing every mission file in `player/missions/` against the registry directly instead of trusting the previous pass's own stated scope. Registered at `heat >= 0.35`, `prob = 1.0`, alongside its Deploy Minefield/Scorched Earth tier-mates.
- [Bugfix] **Final Pass — Planetary Defense Generators Respawned Forever, And Stripped Invincibility From Stations They Never Protected (`cw_planetary_defense.lua`):** three compounding defects in one file. First, cleanup ran on `onRemove()` — which fires whenever the *script* is detached, not only when the generator is actually destroyed (`Avorion_Modding_Codex.md`'s `onRemove()`-vs-`onDelete()` entry) — moved to `onDelete()`, the hook that actually means the object is gone. Second, that same handler blanket-set `invincible = false` on every station in the sector, silently stripping protection from any station that was invincible for an unrelated reason (a vanilla story asset, a DLC entity, another mod's protected structure) — now each protected station is marked with `cw_pdg_protector_id` at grant time (only if it wasn't already invincible), and only stations carrying *this* generator's own mark are restored on removal. Third, and most severe: because cleanup never fired for any destruction that wasn't literally the Shield Breaker mission's own polling check, the faction's `cw_defense_generator_sector` commissioning flag was never cleared for any other destruction path — so the very next player to enter that sector would silently rebuild the generator, forever, regardless of how it was actually destroyed. `onDelete()` now clears the flag generically for every destruction path (Shield Breaker's own explicit clear on mission completion still runs too; both are idempotent). `WIKI.md`'s "Known limitation" paragraph, which described the *opposite* of this behavior, is corrected below. Also corrected the destruction broadcast's `ChatMessageType` from `Error` to `Warning` (matching its own "WARNING:" text) and added its missing `%_T` marker.
- [Bugfix] **Final Pass — A Subspace Corridor Could Announce Itself Then Never Appear (`cw_siege_injector_persistent.lua`):** materialization checked "does this sector contain any `EntityType.WormHole`" as a proxy for "did I already create this corridor's endpoint here" — but vanilla generation places wormholes in a great many sectors independent of this mod, so a faction home sector with a pre-existing vanilla wormhole false-positived as already-materialized and silently never got its own corridor endpoint, after the news article announcing the corridor had already gone out. Replaced with a dedicated per-sector `cw_corridor_materialized_X:Y` marker, set only when this exact corridor actually creates its own wormhole entity, unambiguous regardless of what else the sector contains.
- [Bugfix] **Final Pass — A Near-Zero War Heat Could Misparse As Maximum War Heat (`cosmicwarbridge.lua`):** `publishWarHeatSnapshot()` published each faction's heat via plain `tostring()`; Lua renders a very small float in scientific notation (e.g. `1.2e-005`), and `getWarHeatSnapshot()`'s parse pattern (`(%d+):([%d%.]+)`) stops at the `e`, misreading a heat value effectively at zero as ≥ 1.0 — the mod's maximum tier, unlocking its three hardest contracts and the permanent-wormhole roll for a faction barely past the rivalry threshold. Reachable whenever `cw_war_bias` is near zero and relations sit just past the threshold. Fixed by publishing with `string.format("%.6f", heat)`, which never enters scientific notation regardless of magnitude.
- [Bugfix] **Final Pass — In-Game Codex Cited A Nonexistent File And Two Articles Duplicated The Same Topic (`infoCw.lua`):** the War Contracts article cited `data/scripts/entity/bulletinboardmissions.lua`, which does not exist in vanilla or this mod (the real file is `missionbulletins.lua`), and its own file list was missing five of its own documented contracts (Deploy Minefield, Propaganda Broadcast, Assassinate General, Supply Line Raid, Extract POW — all already named in its prose, just absent from the citation list). The Dynamic War Events article had the same stale-path defect (`eventscheduler.lua` → the real `cw_eventscheduler.lua`) and was missing two entire event scripts with zero Codex coverage anywhere (`cw_headhunters.lua`, `cw_wreckagefield.lua`) plus the Eclipse Vanguard boss encounter, also completely undocumented. ### ⚖️ Numbers & Balance

- [Balance] **Three Highest-Tier Contracts Realigned:** `cw_assassinate_general.lua` and `cw_supply_line_raid.lua` (0.80 War Heat gate) used the 0.25-tier formula (`100,000 + heat·150,000`, copy-pasted from `cw_borderskirmish.lua`); both now use `180,000 + heat·220,000`, matching Blockade Runner, the nearest same-tier anchor. `cw_extract_pow.lua` (1.00 gate — the maximum in the mod) used the same underpriced formula, making it the single worst-paying contract in the game despite requiring maximum war heat; now uses `250,000 + heat·300,000`, matching Champion Duel. - [Balance] **Eclipse Vanguard Hull Toughness (`cw_eclipse_vanguard.lua`):** Carried +49x fire rate and +49x shield (50x total each) but zero hull scaling, so it died like a stock ship the moment its shield broke. Added a 4x `Durability.maxDurabilityFactor` multiplier — enough for a real second phase without approaching Decapitation Strike's true-superboss toughness.
- [Balance] **Capital Ship Duel / Station Siege "Dreadnoughts" (`cw_capital_ship_duel.lua`, `cw_stationsiege.lua`):** Carried the "Dreadnought" title with no size or toughness premium over a default military ship, unlike Stranded Flagship's correctly-scaled 25x-volume dreadnought. Both now spawn at 8x volume with a 5x shield multiplier (`addBaseMultiplier(StatsBonuses.ShieldDurability, 4.0)`) — the same shield multiplier `siegeevent.lua`'s own Siege Dreadnoughts already use, at a volume tier distinct from (and below) the true endgame flagship.
- [Balance] **Warbonds — Global Pool Cap + Outcome-Scaled Return (`tradingpost.lua`, `cosmicwar_warbonds.lua`):** The 300%-or-nothing binary return is now a smooth scale from 0% (famine rose 150+ during the hold — the faction was effectively broken) to 300% (famine held steady or improved), based on the actual `famineDelta` at maturity. A new 1,000,000,000 Cr global pool cap per faction (tracked server-wide, alongside the existing unchanged 250,000,000 Cr per-player cap) now bounds total multiplayer-server investment in one faction's war outcome; the pool frees back up as bonds mature, win or lose.
- [Feature] **Warbonds — Early Cash-Out:** Bonds previously had exactly one exit — hold until the war fully resolves, however long that takes, for whatever the famine-scaled payout happens to be at that point. A new "Cash Out Warbonds Early" Trading Post interaction lets a player exit immediately for a flat 40% return — a real penalty versus holding to maturity, but a guaranteed floor no held bond has, and gives players an actual way to cut losses on a war that's clearly going badly instead of no choice at all.
- [Balance] **Orbital Bombardment Now Actually Bombards (`cw_orbital_bombardment.lua`):** Previously spawned 5 "bombers" that just fought like any other ambush — the file's own comment admitted there was no bombardment mechanic. Rewritten as a persistent event: bombers now periodically inflict direct damage on the defender's stations (`Durability:inflictDamage`, the same API vanilla's own `laserbossbehavior.lua` uses for its boss barrage) — roughly 18%/minute to a random defending station if left completely unchallenged — with an 8-minute withdrawal timer and full `secure()`/`restore()` save-safety.
- [Balance] **Hero of the People Now Pays A Real Reward (`cw_refugeeconvoy.lua`):** The prior "1-hour status" flag had no mechanical effect (see above) because no per-player trade-price hook exists anywhere in Avorion's exposed API — confirmed via the API stubs before concluding this; building one would mean patching vanilla's own trading UI price calculation directly, a systemic vanilla override this suite avoids by design. Replaced with a direct, honest credit reward (300,000 Cr per surviving transport, via the same `player:receive()` mechanism used for every other payout in this mod) instead of an hour-long flag promising nothing concrete.
- [Balance] **Extended Pass — Frontline Siege / High-Value Extraction Paid 3-4x Their Own Tier (`cw_frontlinesiege.lua`, `cw_highvaluedefection.lua`):** Both shared a gate with contracts of comparable scope and duration (Frontline Siege at 0.60 alongside Hunter Killer/Distraction Carnage; High-Value Extraction at 0.80 alongside Blockade Runner/Subspace Containment) yet paid roughly 3-4x their own tier-mates. Frontline Siege realigned from `375,000 + heat·1,125,000` to `250,000 + heat·400,000`, anchored to Distraction Carnage's shape (both are wave-survival missions at the same gate). High-Value Extraction realigned from `750,000 + heat·750,000` to `220,000 + heat·260,000`, anchored to Blockade Runner. Both remain their tier's premium pick without dwarfing their own siblings.
- [Balance] **Extended Pass — Eclipse Vanguard's Offense Now Exceeded The Mod's Own Calibrated Superboss (`cw_eclipse_vanguard.lua`):** This pass's hull fix (above) deliberately kept Eclipse Vanguard's toughness below Decapitation Strike's ("without approaching the true superboss's toughness" — 4x vs 8x hull, correctly done), but its pre-existing offense multipliers (+49x fire rate, +49x shield) were left untouched by that fix — a solo, escort-less ship was hitting roughly 4x harder and tanking roughly 6x more than Decapitation Strike (8 escorts, ~12x fire rate at max heat). Recalibrated using the same `hpMult`-scaling formula Decapitation Strike already uses, evaluated at Eclipse Vanguard's own 4.0 hull multiplier: `+5.0` FireRate (6x total, down from 50x) and `+3.0` ShieldDurability (4x total, down from 50x) — a step below the superboss on every axis instead of above it on two of three.
- [Balance] **Extended Pass — Relief Convoy's Famine Decay Reduced 40 → 20, And Closed A Warbonds Exploit It Enabled (`cw_relief_convoy.lua`, `cosmicwar_warbonds.lua`, `tradingpost.lua`, `cosmicwarbridge.lua`):** A single Relief Convoy delivery undid 8-20 lost sieges' worth of Famine (+5/+2 each) and more than losing an entire war decisively (+15) — reduced to -20, matching Cosmic Chronicles' own decay-event convention (Market Boom -20, Stock Market good roll -25) instead of standing as the largest single decay value in the suite. Separately, and more seriously: a player could buy Warbonds on a faction, then personally fly that same faction's Relief Convoy (or the new Diplomatic Aid Package, below) to manufacture the "the war went well" famine reading Warbonds' payout depends on, independent of how the war actually went. Closed by adding `CosmicWarBridge.recordFamineReliefApplied()`/`getFamineReliefApplied()` — every Humanitarian Contract now records its famine relief to a running, never-reset total; Warbonds snapshots it at purchase and adds back whatever relief was applied during the hold when computing the payout's `famineDelta`, so relief actions still do their real job of lowering the faction's live Famine Score everywhere else, but can no longer be read as a war outcome for bond-payout purposes specifically.
- [Balance] **Extended Pass — War Score's Decisive Victory Was Far Easier To Reach Via Kill-Farming Than Territory (`cosmicwarbridge.lua`):** Territory is weighted 25x a kill specifically so it should "outweigh a long kill streak" (the code's own stated intent), but any valid military kill between two warring factions counts toward it galaxy-wide and uncapped, including ambient AI-vs-AI combat from this mod's own background events — not just player action. Reaching the 250-point Decisive Victory threshold via kill volume alone was realistically far more accessible than via 10 net territory swings, inverting that intent. `getWarScore()` now caps kills' contribution to the combined score at ±100 — kills still move the visible score right up to that cap, but a real territory swing is now always required to cross 250.
- [Balance] **Final Pass — Three More Tier Inversions Found And Realigned:** the earlier tier-realignment passes above corrected the contracts they'd found; a full re-derivation of the reward table against each contract's own War Heat gate found three more. **Propaganda Broadcast** (`cw_propaganda_broadcast.lua`, 0.45 gate) paid identically to Border Skirmish (0.25) and Deploy Minefield (0.35) — realigned `100,000 + heat·150,000` → `150,000 + heat·200,000`, matching its real tier-mates (Deniable Raid, Resource Sabotage). **Hunter Killer** (`cw_hunter_killer.lua`, 0.60 gate) paid less than three separate 0.45-gate contracts — realigned `125,000 + heat·150,000` → `175,000 + heat·225,000`, matching Shield Breaker's shape. **Sensor Deployment** (`cw_sensor_deployment.lua`, 0.15 gate — the mod's *lowest* War Heat gate) paid identically to three 0.35-gate contracts — realigned `100,000 + heat·125,000` → `85,000 + heat·110,000`, a modest step above its real 0.15-tier mate Force Recon rather than matching contracts unlocked at more than double its own threshold. All three fixed in both `initialize()` and `getBulletin()` in the same pass, closing off the exact "landed in one path, not the other" failure class the Assassinate General/Supply Line Raid/Extract POW fix above needed a second pass to catch.
- [Documentation] **Final Pass — Decapitation Strike's ~13x Premium Documented As Intentional (`cw_decapitationstrike.lua`):** flagged as "defensible but undocumented" during this pass's own re-audit. It's the mod's one true superboss fight — up to 8x toughness via `hpMult`, an up-to-8-ship defender escort at max heat — and the premium over Extract POW/Champion Duel (its own 1.00-tier mates) is meant to reflect that. - [Feature] **Intelligence Network (`/cosmicwarintel`):** Force Recon (+25), Sensor Deployment (+35), and Black Box Retrieval (+30) War Contracts now bank Intel Points against their target faction on completion, tracked per-player. `/cosmicwarintel` with no argument lists your banked Intel; `/cosmicwarintel <faction name>` spends 50 Intel against an Imperialist faction to preview its likely next expansion heading (a live query of the same directional-walk algorithm that drives real expansion, seeded deterministically per 15-minute window so every player asking about the same faction gets the same answer). A non-Imperialist faction still consumes the Intel but honestly reports it has no discernible expansion pattern to leak.
- [Feature] **Humanitarian Contracts:** Two new non-combat, non-warlike mechanics give Famine a real player-facing path downward for the first time — previously Famine only ever accumulated (siege losses, Ascendancy's World Eater, Chronicles' bad stock-market rolls) with Chronicles' own market events as the only existing decay path.
  - **Relief Convoy** (`cw_relief_convoy.lua`): a new mission, gated on a faction's Famine Score (≥50, "Struggling" or worse) rather than War Heat. Gather 3,000–8,000 units of a distance-tiered raw material and deliver it to the giver's own sector; completion reduces that faction's Famine Score by 20 (reduced from 40 during the extended pass — see Numbers & Balance below) and pays `50,000 + famineScore·800` credits plus 8,000 reputation. No war declaration, no enemy, no abandon penalty — this is charity work, not a War Contract.

Grants 2 hours of immunity from Diplomatic Sanctions pressure (`cosmicwardiplomaticsanctions.lua` now checks and skips immune factions), implemented as an instant brokered deal rather than a travel mission since sanctions pressure is an ongoing background roll, not a discrete state to resolve at a location.

Visible by hovering a conflict row in the Galactic Politics tab. Feeds two new mechanics:
  - **War Exhaustion** (`cosmicwarceasefires.lua`): ceasefire chance rises +3% per full day a war has been active, capped at +30%, tracked from a new `cw_war_started_<pair>` timestamp set the moment `cosmicwardiplomacy.lua` first declares a war.
  - **Decisive Victory** (`cosmicwarceasefires.lua`): once a pair's War Score magnitude reaches 250, the war ends outright regardless of the normal détente roll — the losing side takes a 15-point Famine Score penalty, relations are forced to a solid peace level, the pair's War Score resets to zero, and a "Decisive Victory" article publishes to the Galactic News Network.
- [Feature] **Coalition Ceasefires:** The existing single-pair "Eclipse Sanitization Protocol" (`cosmicwarcontroller.lua`) generalized into a real, galaxy-wide mechanic: once Cosmic Ascendancy's Eclipse Threat Economy (`eclipse_threat`) crosses 5,000 (on its 0–10,000 scale) while the Eclipse is fully awake, every currently-warring AI faction pair galaxy-wide is pulled into a 1-hour temporary truce — relations pushed clear of the rivalry threshold and new escalation (`cosmicwarcontroller.lua`'s `applyWarPressure`) suppressed for the affected pair for the duration. Runs on its own 30-minute cooldown, independent of the normal per-pair ceasefire roll, and publishes a "Coalition Ceasefire Declared" article naming every pair pulled in. Scoped to factions actually at war ("affected factions"), galaxy-wide reach rather than proximity-limited.

### 🔎 Full Audit Pass

A closing compliance sweep against this mod's own audit protocol: registry set-difference in both directions (missions vs `missionbulletins.lua`, events vs `cw_eventscheduler.lua`, background scripts vs `galaxy/server.lua`, in-game Codex articles vs the files they cite — all clean, 100% registered both ways, every cited file path confirmed to exist), a full Detail Sweep on every file touched by the New Mechanics Pass (the least independently-reviewed code in the mod), Changelog claim spot-checks, and a multiplayer/Alliance/Linux pass. Found two real defects in that newest code:

- [Bugfix] **Occupation & Insurgency Never Actually Activated (`cosmicwarbridge.lua`, `trooptransport.lua`):** `getOccupationData()` parsed the stored `oldFactionIndex,newFactionIndex,endTime` marker with an integer-only pattern (`%d+` for all three fields), but `endTime` is derived from `Server().unpausedRuntime` — a `double`, confirmed against the API stubs — which renders with a decimal point in `tostring()` far more often than not. The anchored pattern failed to match the instant `endTime` carried a fraction, silently returning `nil` unconditionally. Since both the Insurgency raid roll and the Occupation reward penalty are gated on this exact function, the entire mechanic was inert from the moment it shipped in this same pass. Fixed at the write site (`trooptransport.lua`'s `captureStation()`) with `math.floor()` before storing — sub-second precision on a 6-hour window was never meaningful anyway.
- [Bugfix] **Supply Lines' Two Sector Markers Could Leak Into A Later, Unrelated Siege (`siegeevent.lua`, `cw_supply_convoy.lua`):** both `cw_supplyline_scaled_<x>:<y>` (the distance-scaling guard) and `cw_supplyline_cut` (the convoy-destroyed win condition) were keyed only by sector coordinates, with no identity tying either to a specific siege instance. A sector that hosts a second, independent siege later — through the same reconquest system this mod and Cosmic Ascendancy already share — would inherit a stale "already scaled" flag (silently skipping legitimate distance scaling for the new siege) or, worse, a stale "supply line already cut" flag (resolving a brand new siege as an instant, unearned defender win the moment it's checked). Both markers are now keyed by the contested zone's own `startTime`, unique per siege instance; the convoy's `zoneStartTime` is passed through its `addScriptOnce` init argument and persisted via a new `secure()`/`restore()` pair, matching `trooptransport.lua`'s own established pattern for the same class of problem.
- [Bugfix] **Frontlines' "Roughly Double" Patrol Claim Was Imprecise:** the actual numbers (35% spawn chance vs 20%, 5-10 ships vs 3-7) are meaningfully less than double on the ship-count side. Corrected to state the real figures, matching what `WIKI.md` already said correctly.
- [Bugfix] **Letters of Marque Revocation Used An Unreliable Self-Removal Pattern (`cosmicwar_mercenary.lua`):** the commission-revocation fix called a bare self-targeted `player:removeScript("cosmicwar_mercenary.lua")` with no paired `terminate()` anywhere in the file — exactly the pattern `Avorion_Modding_Codex.md` already documents as unreliable (a self-`removeScript()` is deferred, not a proven substitute for `terminate()`'s documented self-removal guarantee) after a prior real incident elsewhere in this codebase. ### 🔍 Fresh-Eyes Review Pass - For Future Reference When Looking Back!

- [Bugfix] **Frontlines Sector Lookup Could False-Positive On A Sector That Was Never A Frontline (`cosmicwarbridge.lua`):** `updateFrontlines()` published every currently-warring pair's border sectors as one flat list, joining each `x,y` coordinate to the next with the same comma already used *inside* each pair (`"x1,y1,x2,y2,..."`). `isFrontlineSector()`'s substring check inherited the ambiguity: a query sector that was never actually recorded could still match by landing on the digit seam between two unrelated, adjacent entries — e.g. entries `1,2` and `3,4`, joined as `1,2,3,4`, wrongly match a query for `(2,3)`. Fixed by separating entries with `;`, a character never used inside a coordinate, so `isFrontlineSector()` now searches for a boundary-anchored `;x,y;` instead of an ambiguous `,x,y,`.
- [Bugfix] **Armistice Escort's Negotiated-Peace Discount Could Never Fire (`cosmicwarceasefires.lua`):** `cw_armistice_escort.lua` sets its one-shot flag as `cw_armistice_<left>_<right>` (underscore, matching `cosmicwarbridge.lua`'s own canonical `warScorePairKey` convention), but the Decisive Victory block that's supposed to read it back and discount the loser's Famine penalty from +15 to +5 checked `cw_armistice_<left>:<right>` (colon) instead. ## [v3.0.10]

### Fixed & Refactored

- **Architecture Audit & Cleanup (Part 2)**: This update is a follow-up to the v3.0.9 release, catching 13 background and entity scripts that were missed during the initial audit.
- **Event Routing Desync Fix**: Widespread architectural violation resolved. Previously, scripts like `cosmicwardiplomacy.lua`, `cosmicwarnews.lua`, and `cosmicwarcontroller.lua` were defining global wrappers for standard engine callbacks (`initialize`, `updateServer`, `getUpdateInterval`), which shadowed the native namespace hooks and caused catastrophic event routing failures under the hood. All illegal global wrappers have been permanently stripped. These scripts now natively bind to the Avorion engine via their official module namespaces as intended.
- **Namespace Injection Compliance**: Repositioned `getUpdateInterval` within `rebuildstations.lua` to be properly encapsulated inside its module namespace, ensuring correct VFS overwrite behavior.

## [v3.0.3]

### 🐛Bug Fixes, ⚖️Balancing and ⚙️Adjustments

- [Bugfixed] **Eclipse Vanguard Shield Bug:** Fixed a syntax error where the Vanguard Boss was assigned a non-existent `shieldMultiplier` property instead of correctly multiplying its actual `shieldMaxDurability`, leaving it vulnerable. - [Balancing] **Siege Troop Transports:** Troop Transport boarding timers now scale dynamically with the defending station's max durability. Instead of a flat 60 seconds to board, transports now require an additional 1 second per 100,000 HP of the station's hull (capped at 5 minutes), giving defenders realistic time windows to defend massive fortified citadels before they are annexed.
- [Balancing] **Dynamic War Economy Scaling:** Freelance Mercenary Payouts and High-Profile War Bounties are no longer flat rates! Both systems have been completely rebalanced to scale exponentially based on the faction's distance to the Galactic Core. Fighting for an Outer Rim faction will still yield standard rates, but shedding blood for inner-core empires will yield massive payouts scaling up to **26x** the base rate.
